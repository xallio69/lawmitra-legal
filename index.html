What is Lawmitra?

Lawmitra is an offline Indian legal-information app. Speak or type your question in Hindi or English, and Lawmitra answers from verified Indian law — BNS, BNSS, and BSA — using an on-device AI model. Your questions never leave your phone.

  • Voice-first: Ask in Hindi or English, out loud.
  • Fully offline: After a one-time AI model download, the app works with no internet connection.
  • BNS/BNSS/BSA verified: Answers grounded in current Indian criminal and consumer law.
  • No accounts, no tracking: Nothing is collected or uploaded.
  • 10 free queries, then ₹149 once — no subscription, ever.

Lawmitra provides general legal information, not legal advice. For advice on your specific situation, consult a qualified advocate.

Common Questions

Why does the app need to download 2.6 GB on first launch?
Lawmitra runs a full AI language model on your device so your questions never leave your phone. This is a one-time download. Once complete, no internet is needed.

Does the app work without the internet?
Yes — fully. After the initial model download, Lawmitra is completely offline.

Is my data private?
Completely. We collect no personal data. See our privacy policy for full details.

What law does Lawmitra cover?
Indian criminal and procedural law under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS), and Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA), plus general consumer rights information.

I purchased but lost access — what do I do?
Tap "Restore Purchase" inside the app. If the issue persists, email us at mayank@justseeitall.com.
