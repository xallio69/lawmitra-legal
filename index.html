Local document storage and cloud analysis are different parts of the experience.

  1. 1

    Bring it onto your phone

    Choose a file, select a photo or scan paper. Original copies and extracted text are kept in the app’s local case storage.

  2. 2

    Review and give permission

    Correct the facts and allow analysis for the case. An internet connection is needed when you ask OpenAI to analyse the text.

  3. 3

    Keep the answer with the case

    The response returns to the app. Our relay does not save case text or answers; it keeps numerical usage records.

OpenAI processes the shared text and has its own retention rules. This service does not promise Zero Data Retention. Local case files are excluded from the app’s device backups, so keep your originals separately.

Before you try it

Can I download Lawmitra now?

Not yet. It is in private testing. This page will link to the verified App Store listing when the public release is available.

Does everything work offline?

Saved cases, local document extraction and PDF brief creation work on the phone. AI analysis needs a connection. A photo or file kept only in iCloud may also need to download before the app can read it.

Does Lawmitra store my documents on its servers?

The private build does not upload original files to our relay or store case content there. With consent, extracted text and case context are processed by our relay and OpenAI. Provider retention, support emails and numerical usage records are explained in the privacy notice.

What will it cost?

Pricing is being evaluated using actual result quality and model usage. There is no public paid offer yet. The earlier ₹149 lifetime proposal is not the offer for this cloud-based product.

Can I recover a case if I lose my phone?

The current private build has no case sync or cloud restore. Keep original documents outside the app. An exported PDF brief is readable, but it is not a restorable backup of the case.

How do I contact someone?

Visit support or email mayank@justseeitall.com. Please describe the technical issue without attaching private case documents.
