Help with the private-test build

Let’s get your case working again.

For technical help, email mayank@justseeitall.com. This is developer support, not legal representation.

Keep your case details private

Send the device model, iOS version, what you tapped and the error message. Do not attach agreements, identity documents, payment credentials or screenshots containing private case details.

Getting started

  1. Choose a case category available in your private build, or open the fictional rental example.
  2. Add documents from Files, Photos or the camera. Review the extracted text against the originals.
  3. Review your role, location, events, goal and corrections.
  4. In Consultation, read the sharing details and choose whether to allow analysis.
  5. Ask a question. Keep the app open while it processes. Read the evidence, missing information and limitations with the answer.

Document and camera problems

The camera does not open

Check the Camera permission for Lawmitra in iPhone Settings. If scanning is unavailable, choose a photo or a file instead. Camera access is only needed for scanning.

A photo or file will not load

Open it in Photos or Files first so any cloud-only copy can download. Use an unlocked PDF or a JPEG, PNG or HEIC image. Check available device storage.

The text is missing or an amount looks wrong

Use a clear, well-lit English document. The current OCR is evaluated for English. Check the original and add a separate correction in Review case facts. Do not treat extracted names, dates or amounts as verified facts automatically.

I reached a document limit

A case supports up to 5 documents, 20 total pages and 60,000 extracted text characters, with a 20 MB limit per file. Choose only the relevant documents or pages. The app rejects over-limit imports instead of silently dropping text.

Analysis and saved answers

There is an unfinished request

A timeout or stopped request may already have reached the provider. The app does not automatically repeat it. Read the unfinished-request notice before deciding to ask again. In private testing, access can also expire; contact the developer for renewed test access.

An earlier answer is marked outdated

Your facts or documents changed after that answer. Review the current case and ask for an updated assessment before relying on earlier next steps.

Why does an answer say legal review is pending?

The private reference pack has not yet received independent practising-advocate approval. A matching quotation confirms where text came from; it does not establish that the rule is current or applies to your facts.

Export, deletion and recovery

How do I share a case?

Open Overview and choose Export case brief. Review the scope notice and choose a destination in the iPhone share sheet. The PDF includes case facts and the latest saved assessment; it does not attach the originals or provide a restorable backup.

Can support restore a deleted case?

The current build does not have cloud case storage or restore. We cannot recover a local case from a server copy. Keep originals separately and check a PDF export before deleting anything important.

A removal is unfinished

Unlock the iPhone, reopen the case and use Finish removing files. If it still fails, note the error and contact support without including document contents.

Accounts, purchases and deletion

Public account access and purchases are not enabled yet. The following describes the prepared account-enabled build; these controls may be unavailable in your current private build.

Will signing in restore my cases?

No. Sign in with Apple is for account and eligible paid-access recovery. Cases, documents and saved answers stay on the device. Signing out keeps them there. A new phone or a reinstalled app cannot recover them from Lawmitra’s server.

A purchase finished but access is missing

Use the original Lawmitra account and the app’s purchase-recovery control. Do not buy again merely because verification is delayed. If the app cannot match a transaction, contact support with the displayed error; do not send your Apple password, card details or case documents.

A paid reply was interrupted

Check the app’s delivery-recovery panel. The service does not keep a copy of the answer to resend, and the app does not automatically buy or request another reply. An access unit is used only after the phone confirms saving the reply. Some unresolved deliveries require app support; share the displayed request reference and technical problem, not your case text. Releasing a held access unit is not an App Store refund.

How do I delete my account and local cases?

In Account access, choose “Delete account and local cases.” Read the confirmation and export a case brief first if needed. This removes paid-access recovery and, after server confirmation, the cases and documents saved on that phone. It cannot be undone. If cleanup is interrupted, unlock the phone and choose “Resume account deletion.” Keep the app installed until local cleanup finishes.

Signing out or deleting the app is not the same as completing account deletion. Apple access revocation and RevenueCat purchase-record deletion run separately and may take time. Account deletion does not automatically refund a purchase. The privacy notice explains records retained during cleanup and the remaining retention work before launch.

Availability and billing

Lawmitra is not yet publicly available on the App Store. There is no public price or active public purchase offer. For an Apple charge, check your Apple purchase history. Apple provides a refund-request process; eligibility and approval are determined by Apple. Never send card information or an Apple password to Lawmitra support.
