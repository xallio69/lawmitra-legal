Privacy, explained by where the information goes.

Your original documents stay on your iPhone. If you allow analysis, extracted text and case context leave the phone so OpenAI can process them. These are different promises.

Before public launch

This notice describes the current private-test app and this website. Public accounts, purchases and final retention arrangements are still being prepared. This is not a claim that those features are already operating.

Who to contact

Lawmitra is developed by Mayank Kutmutia. For privacy questions about the app, email mayank@justseeitall.com. Please do not include a case file, identity document or payment credential in your first message.

What stays on your device

The app stores your cases, original document copies, extracted text, corrections, questions, saved responses and sharing choices locally. It uses iOS file protection and excludes its case files from device backups. There is currently no case sync or cloud restore.

PDF and image text recognition happens on the phone. Camera scans become local PDFs. The photo picker gives the app the item you select, without granting it your whole photo library. Apple may download a selected iCloud-only item before handing it to the app.

What is sent for an analysis

After you allow OpenAI analysis for a case and ask a question, the app sends the reviewed facts, location you entered, separate corrections, relevant extracted document text, your question and conversation context through Lawmitra’s relay to OpenAI. This text can contain names, addresses or other sensitive details from your case. It is not automatically anonymised.

The app sends page references using opaque document identifiers, rather than uploading the original PDFs or images to the analysis service. Connections use HTTPS. Encryption in transit does not prevent the relay or OpenAI from reading the text needed to process your request.

What our relay retains

The current relay processes case text and model responses in request memory. It is configured not to save them to its database or request logs. It keeps request identifiers, a hashed private-tester identifier, request status, timestamps, token counts, duration and cost figures for usage control and troubleshooting. These records do not contain the question, document text or answer.

Those usage records currently have no automatic expiry and remain until deliberately removed by the developer, subject to any applicable obligations. They are not deleted when you delete a local case. The public service’s retention schedule will be specified before launch. Hosting providers may process connection and operational metadata under their own policies. The current relay runs on Fly.io infrastructure in Singapore.

OpenAI processing and retention

We request a response without persistent response storage. That setting is not the same as Zero Data Retention. OpenAI’s published API policy says abuse-monitoring logs may contain content and are retained by default for up to 30 days, with exceptions for legal requirements or preventing harm. We have not verified a Zero Data Retention arrangement for this service.

OpenAI says API data is not used to train its models by default unless the customer opts in. Account-specific data-sharing controls still require verification before public launch; this site does not promise a special retention or training exemption. Read OpenAI’s API data controls for the current provider policy.

Your choices and deletion

  • You can keep a case locally without allowing AI analysis.
  • “Stop sharing case text” prevents future analysis requests for that case. It cannot recall a request already sent.
  • Removing a document clears its local copy, extracted text and prior consultations that may quote it. Deleting a case removes its local case records and document copies.
  • Deleting the app or losing the phone may lose local cases. Keep your original documents separately.

Local deletion does not erase provider-side records already created or copies you shared elsewhere. Contact the developer with privacy requests; the request will be assessed against the data actually held and applicable requirements. Do not send sensitive documents merely to request deletion.

Exports and other apps

You choose a destination when sharing a PDF case brief. The destination may upload or retain it under its own rules. The brief can contain facts and quoted text. It excludes original document attachments and is not a restorable case backup. Deleting the case in Lawmitra cannot remove a copy already shared.

Support, payments and this website

If you email support, your address and message are processed by the developer’s email service and retained for handling the request. Please avoid sending case documents. There are no public app purchases in the current private build. Payment processing and transaction-data disclosures will be added before paid access is offered.

This website does not use analytics scripts, advertising trackers, document uploads, account forms, cookies set by our code or browser storage. Its walkthrough is local and uses fictional data. GitHub Pages hosts the site; GitHub states that it logs visitor IP addresses for security. See GitHub Pages documentation and GitHub’s privacy statement. Opening external links or email uses the destination service’s policies.

Age and changes

The current private test is intended for adults. It is not described as safe for all ages. This notice will change when the product’s data practices change, with an updated date and appropriate in-app disclosure before new processing is introduced.
