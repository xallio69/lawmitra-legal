Lawmitra Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 July 2026

Short version: Lawmitra does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Your questions never leave your device.

1. What Lawmitra does

Lawmitra is an offline Indian legal-information app. It answers questions about Indian law (BNS, BNSS, BSA) using an on-device AI model. The app supports Hindi and English, and uses your device's microphone only to convert your spoken question to text — a step handled entirely by Apple's on-device speech framework.

2. Data we do not collect

We collect nothing. Specifically:

  • No accounts or registration. You never create a profile or log in.
  • No queries or conversations stored off-device. Every question you ask is processed entirely on your iPhone using the local AI model. Nothing is sent to any server.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. There are no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking frameworks in the app.
  • No location data. The app does not request or use your location.
  • No contact or identity information. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or any other identifier.

3. Speech recognition

When you use voice input, your speech is converted to text using Apple's on-device speech recognition service (iOS SFSpeechRecognizer). Lawmitra does not receive or store the audio or transcription — the converted text is processed entirely within the app on your device. Please refer to Apple's privacy policy at apple.com/legal/privacy for details on their speech service.

4. AI model download

On first launch, Lawmitra downloads a compressed AI model file (approximately 2.6 GB) to your device. This download is a one-time transfer of a static model file from a content-delivery server. No personal data is sent during this download. After the model is stored on your device, all AI inference is fully offline.

5. In-App Purchases

Lawmitra offers a one-time lifetime unlock (₹149) processed entirely through Apple's App Store. We do not see or store your payment information. Apple's purchase and billing policies apply; please review Apple's media services terms for details.

6. Children's privacy

Lawmitra does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because no data is collected, the app is safe for all ages.

7. Legal information disclaimer

Lawmitra provides general legal information only — it is not a substitute for legal advice. Nothing in this app constitutes legal advice, and no lawyer-client relationship is formed by using Lawmitra. For advice specific to your situation, consult a qualified advocate.

8. Changes to this policy

If we ever change our data practices, this page will be updated with a new effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Given that we collect no data, meaningful changes are unlikely.
