What Lawmitra currently does.

Lawmitra is an AI-assisted legal-information product in private testing. This page explains the present scope and limits. Final public-service and purchase terms will be published before launch.

The current scope

Private iPhone builds now include rental and lease, vehicle paperwork, and cyber-fraud response workflows. They organise case facts, read English document text locally, record corrections, request consent-based cloud analysis and save case briefs. Access depends on the private build and test invitation. The legal reference packs and development evaluations have not received independent practising-advocate approval.

Vehicle document review cannot establish clear title, authenticity, vehicle history or a completed registration transfer. The cyber-fraud workflow helps organise evidence and reporting steps; it does not submit complaints or recover money. Court representation, legal filing and lawyer-client services are not provided.

Use the evidence and professional judgement

AI responses can be incomplete or wrong. Check quoted passages against the original document, read stated limitations and confirm applicable current law with a qualified advocate. Lawmitra is not a law firm and does not create a lawyer-client relationship, represent you, file documents or guarantee an outcome.

Do not rely on the app alone for a court deadline, emergency or another time-sensitive legal decision. Seek appropriate professional assistance directly.

The fictional walkthrough

The website’s deposit case and downloadable lease are invented test material, also used in the private app. They describe no real person or property and are not an agreement template. Website tab changes do not contact an AI model or give personalised legal advice.

Your documents and access

Keep originals outside the app. Local case records currently have no cloud restore, and exports are readable briefs rather than restorable backups. Sharing text for analysis is optional and subject to the private-build privacy notice.

Private access is limited to invited testers and may expire or be interrupted. There is no public purchase offer or launch price yet. Earlier offline/lifetime pricing descriptions have been retired.

Before public release

The release requires independent legal review, product and purchase testing, final privacy and service terms, and App Store review. The website will show a verified store link when the app becomes publicly available.

Questions about the product can go to mayank@justseeitall.com. Please avoid sending private case documents.
